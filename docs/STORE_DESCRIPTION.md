# ZeroVisual

**A free and open-source legit visual client for Minecraft 1.21.4 (Fabric).** Visuals, HUD, cosmetics and quality of life — **no cheats**: no ESP, no reach, no hitbox changes, no combat automation.

## Features

**Visuals** — Zoom (mouse wheel to adjust), Freelook, FullBright, CustomSky (galaxy, realistic sky with sunsets, black hole), MotionBlur, Nametags (styled, only where the game itself shows name tags), TargetESP (not through walls), ItemPhysics, Ambience, custom Crosshair, BlockOutline, ViewModel, swing Animations, GlintColor, DamageNumbers, AspectRatio and more.

**Cosmetics** — Cape, Wings, Halo, ChinaHat, FireFlies, Trails, JumpCircles (rings or the logo star), HitParticles, HitSound, KillEffect (incl. a black hole), TotemPop and **Pet** — a 3D star or a mini copy of yourself that runs after you.

**HUD** — Minimap (terrain and your waypoints, no player radar), TargetHUD, Keystrokes with CPS, ArmorHUD, Potions, Cooldowns, Compass, Coordinates, InfoHUD, TotemCounter, ChatHeads, ChatTweaks, LowHealth vignette, Watermark, ModuleList, Notifications. All elements can be moved in the chat screen.

**Utilities** — ClickGUI (Right Shift) with search, profiles and 5 fonts, a custom main menu, Waypoints, AutoSprint, AutoTool (highlights the best tool), Screenshot+ (copy to clipboard), AutoReconnect and an offline account switcher.

Interface in **English and Russian** (follows the game language).

## Installation

1. Install [Fabric Loader](https://fabricmc.net/use/) for 1.21.4.
2. Put ZeroVisual and [Fabric API](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api) into the `mods` folder. [Mod Menu](https://modrinth.com/mod/modmenu) is optional.
3. In game, press **Right Shift** to open the menu.

## Русский

**Бесплатный open-source легитный визуальный клиент для Minecraft 1.21.4 (Fabric).** Только визуал, HUD, косметика и удобства — **без читов**.

Более 50 модулей: свое небо с галактикой и чёрной дырой, миникарта, стильные ники, MotionBlur, питомец (звёздочка или мини-ты), крылья, нимб, плащ, эффекты убийства, TargetHUD, Keystrokes, точки на карте, менеджер аккаунтов и многое другое. Интерфейс на русском и английском.

**Установка:** Fabric Loader 1.21.4 + Fabric API, ZeroVisual в папку `mods`, в игре **Right Shift**.

Официальные сборки подписаны: изменённый клиент показывает «Неофициальная сборка». Исходный код: <https://github.com/ZeroCoder407/ZeroVisual>.
